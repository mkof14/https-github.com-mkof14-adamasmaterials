Overview
--------
CVD materials for cutting inserts, blanks, and wear parts. Extreme hardness
(up to ~100 GPa) and wear resistance for precision machining and long edge life.

Typical specifications (starting points — final grade follows RFQ)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Process                 CVD
Hardness                Up to ~100 GPa
Thermal conductivity    High
Form                    Blanks / coated solutions

Applications
------------
- Cutting inserts
- Precision machining
- Wear parts

What to include in an RFQ
-------------------------
Application, insert geometry or blank size, target hardness / wear life,
coating stack if any, volume (pilot or series), and timeline.
